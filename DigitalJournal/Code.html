Part II · Mindfulness

The Emotion
Compass

Before diving into mood tracking and gratitude journaling, step back and see the bigger picture. Recognise patterns in your emotions and how to regulate them.

This exercise is designed to help you recognise patterns in your emotions, and how to regulate them by getting a bigger picture of the situation.

What are your most recurring emotions, and what triggers them usually? Do you recall the roots of these emotions?

Where do you experience these emotions in your body? Describe without any judgement. What thoughts fuel them?

What might these emotions be trying to tell you? What do you need right now — support, care, reassurance, expression, solution, action?

If your best friend was feeling the same way, what would you tell them? Embrace these emotions as valid and be non-judgemental.

Part II · Reflective Exercises

Self-Compassion
Exercise

Start by taking a few deep breaths. We are practicing creating an inner image of kindness and compassion — a Compassionate Partner who supports you unconditionally.

Name them
Describe them: gender, physical appearance, facial expressions, tone of voice, body posture, and any other details you can imagine.
What is the object they offer you as a gift?
While giving it to you, they speak to you as your well-wisher. What do they say about your challenges, qualities, and future?

Let their kindness fully sink in. Return to this space whenever self-judgement arises.

Part III · Daily Practice

Evening
Reflection

The day is done. A few quiet questions to close the loop — not to judge, but to witness.

What was the best moment of today, however small?

What felt heavy? Is there anything you want to set down before tomorrow?

One word for how today felt. One hope for tomorrow.
