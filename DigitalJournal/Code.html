Core
Values

Values aren't aspirations - - they're the things you consistently show up for, even when it's hard. Naming them brings clarity.

```

List 5 values that feel most true to who you are right now. Not who you want to be - - who you actually are.

For each value, write a sentence about where this shows up in your daily life - - or where it doesn't, and why.
```
