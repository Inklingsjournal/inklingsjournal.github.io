A mental health journal
to regulate, reflect

Most journals give you blank pages. Inklings gives you guided prompts built on psychological principles — the same kind of reflective work done in therapy, adapted for you to do on your own, at your own pace.

It is not a productivity tool or a habit tracker. It is a space to understand yourself: your patterns, your relationships, your emotional responses, and where you actually want to go from here.

Designed and curated by Shreya, a practising clinical psychologist with years of experience sitting with people through exactly these questions.

The brand

Where science
meets self-discovery.

Inklings Journal arrived from a simple observation: the tools that actually help people understand themselves are locked behind clinical settings most people can't access, and the tools that are accessible often don't go deep enough to matter.

We wanted to change that. Not by watering down the psychology, but by making it feel like something you could do with a cup of tea in your own time, in your own way.

Everything we create combines the depth of scientific understanding with the ease of accessing that knowledge in a way that feels natural. Warm and thoughtful, where psychology meets everyday life.

The founder

Built from both
sides of the couch.

Shreya is a practising clinical psychologist whose work sits at the intersection of the self, relationships, and the stories we carry, often without knowing it.

Having been on both sides of the therapeutic relationship, she noticed something: people need a space between sessions. A place to sit with what is shifting, to continue the work on their own terms, without waiting for the next appointment.

That realisation became Inklings Journal. Built to walk alongside therapy, and alongside you, in the ordinary in-between moments of a real life.
