A closer look

Inside the chapters

The Roots of Your Story

Chapter 1 · Part 1 — Reflective Journal

Every person's present is shaped by their past. This chapter invites you to explore your earliest impressions of yourself, your family dynamics, and the defining moments that formed your beliefs, behaviours, and emotional patterns.

Through gentle narrative prompts, you'll begin to see your story not as fixed, but as something you can understand, reinterpret, and ultimately author anew. There's no right or wrong here — only honest exploration at your own pace.

Each sub-section ends with a Reflection Pause — a moment to slow down, integrate, and simply be with what has emerged before moving forward.

Sample prompts from this chapter

"What is your earliest childhood memory that you can remember, and how does that memory make you feel today?"
"Who were the most influential people in your early years? What values or beliefs did they pass on to you — and how do they continue to influence you now?"
"What defining moment changed how you saw yourself or the world around you?"

The Inner World

Chapter 3 · Part 1 — Reflective Journal

This chapter takes you into the deeper layers of the self — the inner roles you inhabit, the idealised self you aspire to, and the shadow self you may have learned to hide. Drawing on psychodynamic frameworks, it helps you name the parts of yourself that operate quietly beneath the surface.

You'll explore your inner roles through a structured rating table — seeing which parts of you are most active in your relationships and daily life. Then you'll be guided to meet both your idealised and shadow selves with curiosity, not judgment.

This is one of the most transformative chapters in the journal, and it's handled with care — gently, slowly, with space to breathe.

Sample prompts from this chapter

"Which inner roles feel most familiar to you? How do they show up in your relationships and everyday behaviour?"
"What aspects of yourself do you tend to keep hidden — from others or even from yourself? What would it feel like to meet those parts with compassion?"
"What does your idealised self look like — and what would it mean to be gentler with the distance between that vision and where you are now?"

The Vision for the Future

Chapter 4 · Part 1 — Reflective Journal

After grounding yourself in the past and present, this chapter turns toward what you want to build. You'll explore your dreams and fears, your vision of possibility, and the beliefs about success that shape what you allow yourself to want.

This chapter ends with a powerful section on legacy and purpose — asking you to sit with the deeper question of what kind of life you are moving toward, and what you want to leave behind.

It connects naturally into the Intention Planner, creating a thread from inner vision to outer action.

Sample prompts from this chapter

"If you trusted yourself completely, what would you allow yourself to want?"
"What beliefs about success have you inherited — and are they actually yours?"
"What legacy do you want to leave — not just professionally, but in how people feel around you?"

Why journaling works

The science
behind the practice

Journaling isn't just cathartic. It is one of the most well-researched self-regulation tools available — with evidence stretching across psychology, neuroscience, and clinical practice.

Inklings Journal was built with this research in mind, translating clinical tools into an accessible, beautiful format that anyone can use — with or without a therapy background.

🧠

Narrative journaling builds coherence

Writing your story in narrative form helps the brain create coherent autobiographical memory — reducing anxiety and increasing self-understanding. It's the basis of several evidence-based therapies.

🫀

Naming emotions reduces their intensity

Research shows that labelling emotions — "affect labelling" — activates the prefrontal cortex and reduces amygdala activity. The Emotion Compass and Mood Wheel are built on this principle.

🎯

Written intentions increase follow-through

Studies consistently show that writing down goals and intentions significantly increases the likelihood of acting on them. The Intention Planner puts this research to work in your daily life.

🌿

Grounding regulates the nervous system

Sensory grounding techniques like the 5-4-3-2-1 Method activate the parasympathetic nervous system, interrupting the stress response and bringing you back to the present moment.
