Our brand

Where science
meets self-discovery.

Inklings Journal is a mental health brand started to bring tools that can become your everyday companion in your growth journey. It is a space where one can seek access to resources that guide emotional growth and help you reach your highest potential.

We believe growth happens through curiosity, creativity, and mindfulness. Everything we create combines the depth of scientific understanding with the ease of accessing that knowledge in a way that feels natural, even joyful.

This is not a clinical space. It is a warm, thoughtful one — where psychology meets everyday life, and where you are invited to know yourself a little more, one day at a time.

"A companion for every stage of your journey — not just the difficult ones."
The Inklings Journal team
Our founder

Built from both
sides of the couch.

Shreya is a clinical psychologist who practices psychodynamic therapy. Her work sits at the intersection of the mind, relationships, and the stories we carry — often without knowing it.

Having been on both sides of the therapeutic relationship, she noticed something that stayed with her: people need a space between sessions. A place to sit with their emotions, reflect on what is shifting, and continue the work of growth on their own terms.

That realisation became Inklings Journal — a way to bring her years of clinical experience into a tool that anyone could access, anywhere, at any time. Not to replace therapy, but to walk alongside it.
