What this is

A mental health journal
to regulate, reflect
and grow.

Inklings Journal is a guided digital journal built on the principles used in psychodynamic therapy. It takes you through a structured journey, at your own pace, in your own words.

It is not about productivity or optimising yourself. It is about understanding yourself. The kind of understanding that comes from sitting with a question long enough to find an honest answer.

It works on any device. Your entries are saved securely to your account so you can return from anywhere, any time. This journal does not expire.

01
Grounded in clinical psychology
Every exercise draws from established therapeutic practice: self-compassion, inner child work, attachment theory, cognitive reframing. Serious tools, made accessible.
02
Structured and flexible
There is a clear path through the journal, but you can start anywhere, return to anything, and move at whatever pace feels right for where you are.
03
Private, safe, and yours
Your entries are saved to your personal account. No one else can see them. Download them anytime. They will always be there.

The brand

Where science
meets self-discovery.

Inklings Journal started from a simple observation: the tools that actually help people understand themselves are locked behind clinical settings most people can't access, and the tools that are accessible often don't go deep enough to matter.

We wanted to change that. Not by watering down the psychology, but by making it feel like something you could do with a cup of tea in your own time, in your own way.

Everything we create combines the depth of scientific understanding with the ease of accessing that knowledge in a way that feels natural. Warm and thoughtful, where psychology meets everyday life.

"A companion for every stage of your journey, not just the difficult ones."
The Inklings Journal team
The founder

Built from both
sides of the couch.

Shreya is a clinical psychologist who practices psychodynamic therapy. Her work sits at the intersection of the mind, relationships, and the stories we carry, often without knowing it.

Having been on both sides of the therapeutic relationship, she noticed something: people need a space between sessions. A place to sit with what is shifting, to continue the work on their own terms, without waiting for the next appointment.

That realisation became Inklings Journal. Built to walk alongside therapy, and alongside you, in the ordinary in-between moments of a real life.
